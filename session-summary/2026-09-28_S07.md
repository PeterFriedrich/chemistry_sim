## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Condensed-formula reading rules** (`parseCondensed`):
  - A heavy atom (capacity ≥ 2) bonds to the one before it.
  - Capacity-1 atoms (H, halogens) attach to the current heavy atom, or wait for the first one (HOCH₂…).
  - `(group)n` makes branches.
  - A heavy element with a count > 1 means a **molecular** formula.
  - C=O fallback: if the chain fails, subsets of the "pendant candidates" (capacity 2, no terminal atoms, with a successor) are tried, smallest subset first. That's how CH₃COCH₃ and CH₃COOH read.
- **Bond orders are unique in a tree:** work leaf-inward (`assignOrders`). An order must be 1–3, and the root must come out at exactly 0.
- **Writer choice** (`mainChain` score, in order): fewest brackets, then most C in the path, then starts with C, then longest, then shortest string, then lexicographic. A pendant O at a path end may be trimmed off, which is how HCOOH is written. The first atom's H goes in front for non-C atoms, or for a C with exactly one H (HO…, H₂N…, HC≡…). A round-trip test over 88 isomers guards this.
- **Hydrocarbon polarity is exactly zero** for every conformation. At each carbon the ideal VSEPR bond directions sum to zero, so the C–H dipoles cancel globally. Heteroatom polarity is conformation-dependent, and the sim uses its zigzag from `geometry()`.
- **Layout (`drawChain`):** the chain runs along x at 2 grid units per bond; branches go ±2 in y, alternating up/down on adjacent atoms; outer atoms sit 1 unit out. With ≤ 6 heavy atoms, every branch off the longest chain is one heavy atom deep.
- **Screenshots of typed inputs:** `tools/verify-sims.js` only shoots defaults. The scratch script I used (`scratchpad/shots.mjs`) fills `#ctl-molecule` and shoots. ESM can't use `NODE_PATH`, so import `/tmp/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs` directly.
