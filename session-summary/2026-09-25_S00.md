## 3. Technical Learnings
- **The Data Booklet prints no R, no molar volumes, no STP/SATP, no Avogadro constant**, and no equations at all. Chemistry 20 gases need an owner-supplied source (DATA_SHEET.md §1.5).
- The booklet's units are g, kJ (for ΔH), kPa, L — chem functions use them directly rather than SI.
- The weak-acid approximation rule (5% / [HA] > 1000 Ka) is printed in the booklet; the `titration` / `ph` sims must follow it.
